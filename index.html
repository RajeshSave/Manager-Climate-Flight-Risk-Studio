Manager Climate & Flight-Risk Studio

Each manager is rated by one or more people. The app combines those ratings and comments into a bandwidth for how much of the team may leave in the next 12 months, and says what is driving it.


Load data

Drop your .xlsx or .csv, or choose a file. One row per rating.
Rows are grouped by manager_id and split by respondent_type (Team Member, Reporting Manager, Peer, Self). Only Team Member rows feed exit risk. Other raters feed the 360 view. Items are matched by column name. 0 and blanks count as missing. Optional: audit_failed, fg_tenure_org_clean, fg_department_clean, fg_gender_clean, fg_age_clean, improvement_comments, strengths_comments, Organization Retention.

Model settings

How a manager’s band is built

  1. Each team member’s ratings become deficits, ((5 − score)/4)γ, so a 1 or 2 counts far more than a 3.
  2. Deficits are averaged with theory-based weights (right). Missing items are filled from the same CDI dimension.
  3. A logistic curve turns this into an exit probability, calibrated so the average equals your base attrition rate. Tenure and comments nudge it.
  4. Weights and imputed values are perturbed 40 times to give each person an uncertainty interval.
  5. For each manager, people are bootstrapped (resampled) 150 times. The band is the spread, widened when the team is small. Only a band is ever shown.
  6. Item means for small teams are pulled toward the organisation mean, so one rater cannot define a manager.

Comments

Comments are read with word lists. They are tagged to a CDI theme and as a strength or a concern. Negation and “no suggestion” answers are handled. Words that signal leaving add risk. English only, and approximate. Review excerpts before you act on them.

Ethics and responsible use

Ratings describe a manager’s climate, not the people who gave them. Results and excerpts are hidden for groups below the minimum size. Never use individual-level scores for appraisal, promotion or termination, and check that survey consent covers this use. Age is off by default and gender is never used. The Portfolio fairness screen flags group gaps.

Path to a supervised model

Log real exits for 12 months. Meanwhile, intent to stay is a weak label (the Portfolio checks the score against it). Then fit a regularised logistic model on exits, using these weights as priors, and judge it with AUC and Brier score on a hold-out period.

Item meanings and weights

Weights run from 0 to 10 and are editable. They reflect how strongly each manager behaviour predicts turnover intent in research on supervisor support, justice, job demands and resources, and self-determination.
